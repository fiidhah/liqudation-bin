Project Background

What is a Liquidation Bin Store?

Understanding the business model in very simple words
📦

1. What is Liquidation?

Big online retailers like Amazon and Walmart receive millions of returned items and overstocked boxes every single week.

Liquidation bin stores purchase these huge pallets at wholesale prices and resell them directly to shoppers at steep discounts.

📉

2. The Daily Price Drop Rule

Instead of different prices for each product, EVERY item in the store costs the exact same flat price on that day:

  • Friday: ₹999 (New stock arrives)
  • Sat: ₹799 • Sun: ₹599 • Mon: ₹399
  • Tue: ₹299 • Wed: ₹199
  • Thursday: ₹99 (Clearance day)

The longer you wait, the cheaper it gets — but someone else might buy it first!

🌐

3. Why We Built SmartBin

Physical bin stores have big drawbacks: customers must wait in long lines, dig through messy bins, and staff must manually change signs.

SmartBin brings this concept online 24/7 so shoppers can search clean categories, view real condition grades, and buy from home.

Workflow

How SmartBin Works (Step-by-Step)

The simple 5-step journey from inventory arrival to customer doorstep
1

Upload Stock

Store Manager intakes pallet stock, uploads product photos, and assigns a Bin ID (Bin A-Z).

2

Auto Pricing

System generates 7-day prices (₹999 down to ₹99) and syncs today's active price in MySQL.

3

Browse & Pick

Customer searches products, views condition ratings, and adds items at today's deal price.

4

Easy Payment

Customer checks out securely via Razorpay. Stock count automatically decrements by 1.

5

Pack & Ship

Manager picks item from bin, ships package, and customer tracks live delivery progress.

Database Design

Database Design (Simple & Organized)

How data is stored cleanly and protected in MySQL

Key Tables in MySQL

users & admins: Stores login credentials, encrypted passwords, and delivery addresses.
products: Stores product title, category, bin ID, condition grade, and photos.
daily_prices: Stores the exact prices for all 7 days (Friday ₹999 to Thursday ₹99).
cart & wishlist: Remembers items added by users for checkout.
orders & order_items: Stores order details, totals, and shipment status.

Safety & Automation Features

• Automatic Cascade Delete (ON DELETE CASCADE):
If a manager deletes a product, MySQL automatically cleans up its daily prices and cart entries. This prevents foreign key errors.
• Midnight Price Auto-Sync:
A background timer runs every minute. When midnight arrives, it automatically updates all product prices to the new day's scheduled price.
• Encrypted Passwords:
All passwords are encrypted using salted bcrypt hashing so user data is always safe.
• Connection Pooling:
Uses MySQL connection pool to handle multiple simultaneous customer checkouts fast and smoothly.
